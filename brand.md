# Alumni Connect Brand Identity

This guide defines the values, voice, and visual direction of the Alumni Tracking System.

## Brand Purpose

Alumni Connect helps Management Information Systems graduates stay connected with one another, discover career opportunities, and keep up with their community.

## Core Values

- **Connection:** Build a lasting bridge between graduates, students, and the wider university community.
- **Opportunity:** Make career information and professional relationships easier to discover.
- **Trust:** Present information clearly, respectfully, and consistently.
- **Progress:** Support lifelong learning and collaboration across graduating classes.

## Audience

- MIS graduates looking to maintain professional relationships.
- Alumni sharing job opportunities or community updates.
- Students and early-career professionals seeking guidance and connections.
- Department staff coordinating announcements and alumni activity.

## Voice and Writing

- Professional, supportive, and welcoming.
- Clear and direct; prefer familiar language over technical jargon.
- Encouraging without overstating what the platform can do.
- Use Turkish for user-facing website copy and English for technical documentation.

## Visual Identity

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight navy | `#1a252f` | Primary text, navigation, and strong surfaces. |
| Alumni orange | `#e67e22` | Primary actions, highlights, and focus accents. |
| Warm paper | `#f7f5f0` | Main page background. |
| White | `#ffffff` | Cards and form surfaces. |
| Muted green | `#49675b` | Secondary labels and supportive status accents. |

### Typography

- **Headings:** Merriweather or a Georgia serif fallback for an editorial, alumni-magazine character.
- **Body and controls:** Inter or a system sans-serif fallback for readability and consistency.
- Keep body copy comfortable to scan; use clear line-height and short paragraphs.

### Imagery and Iconography

- Prefer genuine community photography and simple abstract network motifs.
- Use restrained, consistent line icons; avoid mixing emoji and unrelated icon styles in production UI.
- Provide useful alternative text for informative imagery and hide decorative graphics from assistive technology.

## Accessibility and Inclusivity

- Maintain readable contrast between text, backgrounds, and interactive controls.
- Make every action keyboard-accessible and provide a visible focus state.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate status or meaning.
- Use inclusive language that welcomes graduates across cohorts and career stages.
